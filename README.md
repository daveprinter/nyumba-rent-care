# Nyumba Rent And Care

CREATE A APP CALLED NyumbaLink is one place where landlords, caretakers, and tenants run a rental property together. A landlord lists their houses and the rooms in them, assigns tenants to rooms with a lease, and the app keeps track of who owes what and who has paid. Tenants sign in to see their own rent, pay it, and report problems; caretakers sign in to handle day-to-day duties on the ground.

The parts that matter most:

Rent and payments — monthly bills are generated automatically, payments are recorded against them, and anything unpaid shows up as arrears so nobody has to chase it by hand.

Tenants and leases — every room has a tenant, a start date, and an agreement attached, so you always know who lives where and when a lease ends.

Maintenance requests — tenants raise a fault, and it stays visible until someone closes it out.

Separate views for each role — a tenant, a caretaker, and a landlord each see only what's theirs.At the home tab, where there is the login or create account, add a background, a video, and an animation relevant to what the app does. So when they click send to caretaker or landlord, they automatically appear in the landlord or the caretaker dashboard, both of them. Then they approve. After approving-- now to approve a payment in the tenant, they will enter the code, then it is checked. Also, add the messaging technique for communication between landlord, caretaker, and also the tenants. And property and tenant timelines, like notices and notice history.
